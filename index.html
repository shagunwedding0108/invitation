By Air

Fly to Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar International Airport, Nagpur. Continue by road to the Turia Gate / Jamuntola area.

▰

By Train

Nagpur Junction is a convenient major railway hub. Arrange onward travel by road to the resort.

⌁

By Road

From Nagpur, travel via Khawasa toward Turia Gate, then follow the exact resort pin. The Nagpur–Turia route is approximately 85 km; distance to the resort may vary.
